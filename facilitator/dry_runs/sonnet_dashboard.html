1How is the business really doing?

2What should we worry about?

Warsaw gross margin collapsed Apr-Nov 2025

Gross margin % by city (lines) and Warsaw sausage price, PLN/kg (bars)

Warsaw sausage cost went from ~15 to ~66 PLN/kg in April 2025 and only normalised in Nov. Margin fell from 74% to 63%: about EUR 88k of extra cost. Copenhagen also drifts down ~0.5pt a year because prices were never raised.

C4 Christianshavn: cash is vanishing

Card share of units by Copenhagen stand, %

Since the new manager started (Mar 2025) C4 card sales are unchanged vs sister stands but cash sales fell ~55%, so card share jumped from 70% to 85%. Estimated unrecorded cash: EUR 50-62k in 10 months. Logbook: "Cash drawer sticky again. Will sort it out myself." (10 times). Needs an audit.

Rain kills uncovered stands

Daily revenue vs a dry day of the same stand, month and weekday (index, dry = 100)

On days with 2+ mm of rain, open-air stands sell ~40% less; the covered station stands lose ~5%. Rain days at open-air stands cost roughly EUR 270k of sales over two years.

Stadium stands live off ~14% of days

Average daily revenue, EUR

C3 and W3 earn ~EUR 150 on a normal day and ~EUR 650 on event days; concerts bring ~EUR 1,900. Christmas markets and marathons: nothing. They earn only ~6% of revenue, so check whether staffing a full week is worth it.

3What should we do more of?

Vegan dog is the growth engine

Copenhagen units sold per month

Vegan units +92% in Copenhagen and +87% in Warsaw (2025 vs 2024), now 14% / 7% of units. It eats the red sausage (-36%) and some classic dogs, and gross profit per vegan dog (EUR 4.4) beats the red sausage (EUR 3.4). Push it, and rethink the red sausage.

The new seasonal stand W4 pays off

Warsaw W4 vs W1: avg daily revenue by month 2025, EUR

W4 opened May 2025 and took EUR 196k in 5 months (EUR 1,279/day, gross margin 64% hit by the sausage spike) without hurting W1 (+3.6% May-Sep). Test an April/October season.

Right stand, right day

Avg daily revenue on non-event days by weekday and stand type, EUR

Commuter stands (stations) earn ~EUR 2,050 Mon-Thu but fall 40-50% at weekends; tourist squares gain ~50% on Saturday and the seasonal stand W4 doubles. Staff and stock accordingly; Christmas markets lift C1/W1 by +12%/+22% and cause chocolate milk and coffee to double their share.
Method: sales converted to EUR with the daily rate; margin = (revenue - cost_of_goods)/revenue. Day-level indices compare each stand-day to a dry, event-free day of the same month and weekday. Synthetic data. See findings.md for caveats.
